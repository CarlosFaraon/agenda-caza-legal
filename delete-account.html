Delete account - HuntLog

How to delete your account

To delete your account and all your data, follow these steps inside the app:

  1. Open the HuntLog app
  2. Tap your profile photo (top-right corner)
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the profile screen
  4. Tap the "Delete my account" button (in red)
  5. Confirm the deletion

What data is deleted

When you delete your account, the following is permanently erased:

  • Your profile (name, photo, phone, region)
  • All your hunts
  • Your gear (firearms, dogs)
  • Your documents and expenses
  • Your hunting grounds
  • Your chat messages
  • Your push subscriptions
  • Your login account

This action is irreversible. Once the account is deleted, the data cannot be recovered.

Contact

If you cannot access the app to delete your account, contact us at agendadecaza@gmail.com stating the email you registered with, and we will proceed with manual deletion.
