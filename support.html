Frequently asked questions

  • How do I create an account? When you open the app for the first time, tap "Create account," enter your email and a password, and confirm that you are 18 or older.
  • What is location used for? To show you on the map, record the GPS track of your hunts, and display the weather forecast for your area. It is only accessed with your permission.
  • What is the SOS alert? It lets you notify the hunters in your party of an emergency by sharing your location. It does not replace official emergency services (911).
  • How do I delete my account and data? From your profile inside the app, or see the account deletion page.
